# ICGS137 Witchcraft and Gender Representation

## Course Overview

- This course focuses on:

    - **The history of women's suffering**: how women suffered in the past and how gender oppression continues today.

    - **LGBTQ+ movements and suffering**: identity, social expectations, exclusion, and everyday gender issues.

- The course is not meant to be heavy theory only.

- The professor framed the class as connected to everyday life, opinion, criticism, and reasoning.

> "Most of my exams are based on your opinion, whether you agree or disagree."

## Exam Style

- Exams are mostly **opinion-based**.

- The professor said grades depend on:

    - **logic**

    - **reasoning**

    - quality of explanation

    - useful examples

- A good answer should:

    - understand the major course content

    - use the simple concepts and theories correctly

    - add your own ideas and examples

    - answer the exact question instead of writing everything you know

## Midterm Format

- The midterm is **paper-based** and taken in class.

- It is worth **30 points**.

- It uses **short-answer questions**.

- Short answer can still require:

    - explanation

    - examples

    - opinion

    - comparison

- There is **no word limit**.

- For smaller point questions, answer only what is asked.

- For larger questions, give enough reasoning and examples.

## Question System

- The professor said students will hear or receive exam-style questions before the exam.

- Each lecture may include possible questions.

- The actual exam may choose from a larger collected question pool.

- The consolidated question page lists only questions that were explicitly stated or strongly signaled in the lecture notes.
