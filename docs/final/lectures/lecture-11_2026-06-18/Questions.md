## Confirmed Essay Choices

### Cultural Theories And Gender Issues

- **Question / topic:** Apply **cultural theories** to gender issues, especially environmental determinism and positivism.

- **Explicit signal:** stated as **"there will be a few questions based on these theories."**

- Include:

    - **Environmental determinism** explains culture as arising from nature and treats nature as controlling human life.

    - Environmental determinism is connected to predestination and to arguments that reject self-control or personal responsibility.

    - This can connect to gender violations when people excuse harmful acts as God's will, nature, hormones, or uncontrollable forces.

    - **Positivism** explains culture as arising from collective human experience, knowledge, science, and technology.

    - Positivism can support justice because it uses facts, logic, and reasoning.

    - Positivism can also leave little room for hope, imagination, and human nature.
