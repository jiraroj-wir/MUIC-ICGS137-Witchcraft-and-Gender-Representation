## Essay Items

### Cultural Perspectives

- **Label:** `Choice`

- **Question / topic:** Explain what **conservative**, **liberal**, and **radical** perspectives think about culture; evaluate which side is more reasonable; and identify which side helps reduce or prolong gender issues.

- **Why marked:** stated as **"This is going to be one of your essays"** and followed by **"I will ask you to explain"** conservative, liberal, and radical views of culture.

- Include:

    - Conservatives aim to protect and maintain existing culture.

    - Conservatives believe culture shapes personality, behavior, character, identity, confidence, and success.

    - Liberals and radicals believe people have freedom of choice and can shape their own personality, behavior, and character.

    - Liberals and radicals see culture as flexible and less decisive in forming people.

    - Evaluate which side is more reasonable.

    - Explain which side helps reduce gender issues.

    - Explain which side prolongs gender issues.
