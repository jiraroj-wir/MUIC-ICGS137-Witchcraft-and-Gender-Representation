## Essay Items

### Witchcraft, Fear, And Demonization

- **Label:** `Choice`

- **Question / topic:** For the **witchcraft question**, explain why witches were feared and demonized, starting with Christian condemnation.

- Include:

    - Start with condemnation by the Christian church.

    - Explain how church teaching created a negative image of witches and witchcraft.

    - Explain the fear that witchcraft challenged Christian authority, God's power, and the church's control over followers.

    - Explain that church and political authorities could use witchcraft fear to control people, especially powerful, independent, or non-conforming women.

### Witchcraft Case Study And Intersectionality

- **Label:** `Choice`

- **Question / topic:** Analyze a witchcraft accusation case study using **intersectionality** and identity factors.

- Include:

    - Explain why the accused person becomes a target.

    - Use identity factors such as gender, age, widowhood, class, rural background, healing knowledge, minority religion, non-binary identity, or other markers of difference when the case provides them.

    - Explain how multiple identities combine to increase vulnerability.

    - Show how scapegoating works when a community wants an answer for fear, death, crisis, or uncertainty.
